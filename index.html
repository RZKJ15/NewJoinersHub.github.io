Setup phase

Your first two months

What to focus on, stage by stage, while you're getting set up.

Day 1
First day

Get set up

  • Focus: Get familiar with Devoteam and make sure you have everything you need to get started.
  • Actions: Set up your Devoteam account, install PalGate and Joan, and log into AFAS and Workday.
  • Meetings: Welcome meeting with your manager, meet your Team Coach and team, and meet your buddy (if applicable).
  • HR/People: Complete your Day 1 HR onboarding, review key practical information and employment conditions, and get familiar with your HR/People points of contact.
Week 1
First week

Get connected

  • Focus: Get to know your team, your role and how we work together.
  • Training: Start with the GenAI Level 1 training.
  • Getting to know people: Attend Welcome Day, meet your manager and Team Coach, and start building your internal network.
  • Certifications: Review the certifications relevant to your role and align with your manager on your certification and development path.
Month 1
First month

Find your rhythm

  • Focus: Get comfortable in your role, your project and Devoteam's ways of working.
  • Suggested actions: Have your first onboarding check-in with your manager and Team Coach, clarify your role and expectations, get familiar with your client/project, and identify any areas where you need additional support or training.
  • HR/People activities: Complete your 1-month onboarding check-in, make sure your HR information and benefits are correctly set up, complete any outstanding onboarding activities, and share feedback on your onboarding experience.
Month 2
Two months in

Settle in fully

  • Focus: Build confidence in your day-to-day role, widen your network and start thinking about your development at Devoteam.
  • Suggested actions: Review your progress and first achievements with your manager, discuss feedback from your team or client, set initial development goals, explore relevant learning or certification opportunities, and get involved in communities, initiatives or events that interest you.
  • Meetings: Have your 2-month onboarding check-in with your manager and Team Coach, and discuss your development and next steps.

Looking for guidance beyond month two (first 90 days, first year)? That's out of scope for this version of the hub — ask your People Lead in the meantime.

Finance & pay

Getting set up as a new joiner (NL)

Covered by Marc Bovy, CFO, during Welcome Day.

🪪 BSN & bank account

A BSN (fiscal number) is required. HR (Melissa Posdijk) helps you apply for a fiscal BSN and a Dutch bank account.

💶 Tax advantage & relocation

Silvia Small (HR) applies for the 30% ruling if applicable, after your first salary run — your salary is paid from your start date as if it's already granted. A relocation allowance can be paid on request, generally once you have a Dutch bank account, subject to repayment.

🏦 Pension

You're automatically enrolled in the collective pension scheme with A.S.R. DoenPensioen. You'll get a personal welcome email with instructions within 6 weeks of starting.

🩺 Healthcare

Personal healthcare insurance is mandatory in the Netherlands. Arrange your own, or use Devoteam's collective insurance for slightly lower premiums (intermediary: Schouten Zekerheid — CZ/Menzis). If you're joining from abroad, contact them as soon as possible. Deduction starts after activation, which can take a few months — expect a catch-up charge.

💳 Salary

Payment date is the 25th of each month. Your slip is available in English or Dutch via AFAS InSite and the AFAS Pocket app. Healthcare premiums and pension contributions are deducted monthly, and expenses are paid out with your salary. Silvia Small (HR) is happy to walk you through it any time.

🚗 Commuting & mileage

Lease car: no recurring public transport costs, unless your assignment is far from home. Own car: claim mileage via AFAS. Parking costs are claimable — but be cost-efficient (cheapest, not nearest). Public transport: use your OV-Chipcard and download travel costs/expenses via AFAS. Full detail in the Car & Commuting Allowance policy (an addendum to the Employee Handbook).
